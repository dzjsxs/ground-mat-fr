Product Introduction

High Density Polyethylene (HDPE) Sheet is a versatile thermoplastic known for its excellent chemical resistance, high impact strength, and low moisture absorption. With a density ranging from 0.941 to 0.965 g/cm³, HDPE offers a superior strength-to-density ratio compared to other polyethylene grades.

HDPE sheet is produced through extrusion or compression molding, resulting in a uniform, stress-free structure suitable for precision machining. Its FDA and NSF 61 compliance makes it particularly valuable in food processing and water treatment applications. The material's excellent weldability also allows for the fabrication of complex structures such as tanks, containers, and custom enclosures.
